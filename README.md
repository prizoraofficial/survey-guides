# Research Hub

Research Hub is an educational resource website focused on:

- market research
- digital consumer insights
- paid surveys
- online rewards
- consumer feedback systems

This project provides beginner-friendly educational content explaining how online survey systems and digital research platforms work.

## Topics Included

- Market Research Resources
- Digital Consumer Insights
- Paid Survey Learning Center
- Online Rewards Education
- Survey Participation Tips

## Educational Resources

- https://www.prizora.com
- https://paidsurveys78.wordpress.com/
- https://sites.google.com/view/smart-survey-guide/home

## Why This Project Exists

Businesses use market research and consumer feedback to improve products, services, and customer experiences. This repository helps explain how online surveys and digital consumer research systems operate in modern industries.
